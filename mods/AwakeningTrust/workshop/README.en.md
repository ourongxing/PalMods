# Awakening Trust

0.3.0 · ourongxing

Make Trust and Awakening count for more. Once a Pal awakens, it gets much more out of Trust, helping close the base-stat gap between species and giving your favorites a chance to shine.

## How it works

- The boost starts after Awakening—you don't need to max out Trust first. Higher Trust means a bigger benefit.
- At maximum Trust, Pals whose vanilla HP, Attack and Defense base stats add up to less than 450 are brought up to a total of 450, keeping their original proportions.
- Pals already at 450 or above stay as they are. Strong Pals aren't nerfed.
- Unawakened Pals keep their normal stats. The game's own Awakening bonus still applies.

## How much of a boost?

Here's Cattiva next to Frostallion Noct at maximum Trust, in HP / Attack / Defense order:

- **Cattiva**: 125 / 107 / 107 → about 165.93 / 142.04 / 142.04. Total: **339 → 450**.
- **Frostallion Noct**: 160 / 150 / 146. Already at **456**, so nothing changes.

These are base stats with Trust growth included, rather than the final numbers on the stats screen. The game's own Awakening bonus is added separately.

## Multiplayer

Works in single-player, player-hosted co-op and on Windows dedicated servers. For multiplayer, install the appropriate build on both the client and the server.

## Installation

Subscribe to and enable **UE4SSExperimentalPW**, enable this mod in the game’s Mod Management menu, then fully exit and restart the game.

For Windows dedicated servers, install the same Workshop package and its dependency, then restart the server. A separate server DLL is included.
